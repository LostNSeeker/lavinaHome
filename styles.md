
**Design & Typography Style**

* **Design Aesthetic:** Whimsical, minimal Scandinavian style designed for children's decor. It relies on soft watercolor-like textures, gently rounded corners, and hand-drawn geometric forms (house silhouette, floating hearts, and a friendly teddy bear).
* **Typography:** A friendly, rounded sans-serif typeface (similar to *Quicksand*, *Fredoka*, or custom hand-lettered rounded vectors) where each letter features an alternating color rhythm.
* **Subtext / Tagline:** Set in an uppercase, widely-tracked geometric sans-serif for a clean, modern accent.

**UI & Brand Extension Tips**

* **Primary Buttons & CTAs:** Use the **Sage Green** (`#8EBBB0`) or **Warm Coral** (`#E79685`) as primary action colors against the off-white background to ensure soft, readable contrast.
* **Card & Container Surfaces:** Stick to `#FFFFFF` or very subtle warm tints on a `#FDFBF7` page background rather than harsh stark whites (`#FFFFFF` only for cards to lift them).
* **Font Pairing:** Pair a rounded font for headings (*Fredoka*, *Nunito*, or *Comfortaa*) with a clean, highly legible font like *Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans* for body text and navigation.
